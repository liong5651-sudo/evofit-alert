EVOFIT ALERT Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 15 October 2026

EVOFIT ALERT respects your privacy and allows users to request deletion of personal information associated with our services.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like us to delete personal information associated with EVOFIT ALERT, please send a data deletion request by email.

Email: liong5651@hotmail.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your name.
  • The phone number, email address or social media account associated with your interaction.
  • A brief statement requesting deletion of your EVOFIT ALERT data.

Example Request

Subject: EVOFIT ALERT Data Deletion Request

Please delete personal information associated with my account or interaction with EVOFIT ALERT.

Processing of Requests

After receiving a valid request, we will identify information reasonably associated with the requester and delete or anonymise applicable personal information, except where retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or legitimate business record purposes.

Meta Platform Data

Where information originates from Meta services such as Facebook, Instagram, Threads or WhatsApp Business Platform, users may also manage or remove information through the relevant Meta account and privacy settings.

Confirmation

Where appropriate, we may contact you to confirm completion of the request or request additional information necessary to verify the request.

Contact

For questions concerning data deletion:
